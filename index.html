How this hub works

This site lives on the web so it opens properly on your phone (the old file version needed a real browser to run its scripts, which is why it sometimes looked broken on mobile). The tracker saves your ticks and weights to your own phone or laptop as you go, so if you and Jenna each use your own device, your progress is tracked separately on each one, it isn't synced between you. Log the final numbers in your spreadsheet afterwards if you want one shared record.

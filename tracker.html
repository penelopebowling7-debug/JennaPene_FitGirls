How this works

Pick the week and day at the top. Tap a round chip after you finish that round of an exercise, it fills in pink. Warm up and cool down items are single taps. The weight box is pre filled with the planned load, tap it to change what you actually used. The progress bar at the top of each day fills up as you check things off.

The small circle next to an exercise name opens a how-to video in a new tab. A filled pink circle (▶) means it links straight to a specific video we found for that move. A light circle with a magnifying glass (🔍) means we didn't have a saved link for that one, so it opens a YouTube search for it instead, still useful, just not hand picked.

Every timed block (the ones with a "sec active / sec rest" tempo) has its own Start Round Timer button right under the block name, showing how many exercises are in it. Tap it once at the start of a round and it walks you through every exercise in the circuit in order, work, then rest, then the next exercise, all the way to the end, calling out cues as it goes: "let's go" to start, a warning with 10 seconds of work left, "and rest" at each switch, and "Round complete" once the whole circuit's done, not just one move. When it finishes, it automatically ticks that round's chips for every exercise in the block for you. Tap the button again for the next round. A few blocks are rep based with a rest between them (like the Push Up Block), those pause and say "tap start when you're ready to rest" after each exercise since there's no fixed work time to count down, just do your reps at your own pace then tap. Make sure your phone isn't on silent so you can hear the cues, they use your phone's built in voice, not a saved audio file.

The plain Rest Timer button at the bottom is a manual stopwatch for anything else, tap a preset to set how many seconds to count down, tap Start to begin, and it buzzes and beeps when time is up.

Once every box on a day is ticked, a 🏁 Finish Workout button appears. Tapping it is the only thing that actually archives a workout, just ticking every box doesn't do it automatically, that's on purpose so nothing gets accidentally recorded while you're poking around the app. Confirming asks how long it took (pre-filled with a guess based on when you started ticking, edit it if that's off), how it felt, and an optional note. Once confirmed, that day drops off your to-do list for good and moves into your history on the Fun Stats page, since the plan is to keep building new weeks forward rather than repeating old ones.

Before you finish, tap Copy Summary for a plain text record of what you did, weights included.

Your progress saves automatically and syncs live between your devices — tick something on your phone and it shows up on the laptop or TV within a second or two, no refresh needed, so you can control it from your phone while everyone watches the bigger screen. It's also saved on this device itself, so it's still here if you close the tab or lose signal mid session, and catches up with everyone else's changes the moment you're back online. Tap "Reset in-progress ticks" below if a day's ticks get messed up and you want that one to start fresh everywhere, it won't touch anything you've already finished and archived.

How this works

Pick the week and day at the top. Tap a round chip after you finish that round of an exercise, it fills in pink. Warm up and cool down items are single taps. The weight box is pre filled with the planned load, tap it to change what you actually used. The progress bar at the top of each day fills up as you check things off.

The small circle next to an exercise name opens a how-to video in a new tab. A filled pink circle (▶) means it links straight to a specific video we found for that move. A light circle with a magnifying glass (🔍) means we didn't have a saved link for that one, so it opens a YouTube search for it instead, still useful, just not hand picked.

Use the Rest Timer for your work and rest intervals during circuits. Tap a preset to set how many seconds to count down, tap Start to begin, and it buzzes and beeps when time is up.

Before you finish, tap Copy Summary for a plain text record of what you did, weights included.

Your progress is saved automatically on this device (browser storage), so it's still here if you close the tab or lose signal mid session. If you and Jenna each use your own phone, your ticks are separate on each device, they don't sync to each other. Tap "Reset this plan" below if you ever want to clear everything and start fresh.
